### Exact mapping of adapted / non-verbatim Round 1 assertions

Every Round 1 invariant from `tests/sim.test.mjs:478–614` is preserved biologically; only Round 1 implementation details superseded by Round 3 architecture were adapted:

1. **Single-object `createExpressionSim` API (`update`, `setCutaway`, `setCameraMode`, `focusTargetFor`, `blobs`)** → replaced by stateless `buildStory` / `stateAt` / `chapterAt` / `createPoser` (`{ pose, reset }`) plus `createStoryScene` (`tick`, `setToggles({ cutaway })`, `setCameraOption`, `CameraRails` / `target.focus`, cryo-EM `ActorSpec` list in `target.actors`).

3. **Peptide storage (`asm.residues` with `resPos`/`resVis`/`resOp`/`resBond` and `code3 === 'fMet'`)** → replaced by procedural `peptide` `AtomGroup` in `target.groups` (`count`, `pos`, `visible`, `opacity`, `residues` using PDB CCD 3-letter codes where initiator N-formylmethionine is `'FME'`; `SCENARIO.peptide[0].code3` remains `'fMet'` with `kind === 'initiator'`).

5. **Frame orthonormality bound (`maxOrtho(asm) < 1e-6` → `< 1e-5`)** → Round 1 generated frames analytically in IEEE-754 float64; Round 3 composes track and anchor rotation matrices serialized to 6 decimal places in `assets/*.asset.json` (each matrix has up to `~1.03e-6` quantization error; composed nucleotide frames reach `2.24e-6` and actor frames reach `1.26e-6` across all 121 samples). `< 1e-5` remains 10× tighter than `validateAsset`'s `1e-4` ceiling (`js/assets/format.js:94,129`).
