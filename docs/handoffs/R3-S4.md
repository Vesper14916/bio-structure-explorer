# Handoff — R3-S4 (Translation choreography, chapters 8–16)

Stage: ST7 · Branch: `r3/S4` (base `r3/K3`, which is based on tag `r3-w2`) · Owner: Claude

## Changed files

- `js/sim/gene/translation.js` — Replaced the W0 stub with `contributeTl(story, t, out)`, which turns the translation events of the timeline into WorldState for chapters 8–16:
  - **Layout (§10 R3-S4 layout rule).** `RIBO_LAYOUT` places the `ribo` frame 250 Å along world −z from the mRNA midpoint at the end of chapter 7. +z is the mRNA 5′→3′ tangent at the SD region; +x (toward the 50S) is the component of world −z perpendicular to it, so the 50S faces away from the DNA. The constants are embedded so `stateAt` stays asset-free; the tests re-derive them from the assets.
  - **Machines.** The ribosome machine (id `ribosome`) has parts `small` and `large`. During `ribosome.bind` the 30S, IF1 and IF3 approach along `LARGE_DIR` (260 Å). The 50S slides in during `ribosome.join`, and the ribosome stays fixed once bound. Factors (IF1–3, EF-Tu, EF-G, RF1) fade and move 220 Å along `FACTOR_DIRS` on `factor.enter/leave`, and RF1 retreats 60 Å on release.
  - **tRNA slots.** There are four slots (`slotOf(k) = (k − 1) % 4`) with `from/site/blend`. Extra fields for the poser are `pair` (0..1 from `codon.pair`; 0 in E; let go first when leaving), `approach`, `carrier` (EF-Tu ternary complex) and `depart`.
  - **Codon and peptide.** The P-site codon is fractional during `translocate`. Peptide `length`, `attach` (P/A/free) and `release` are set, with the C-terminal residue added at the bond midpoint.
  - **FX.** A peptide-bond pulse and an RF1 GGQ hydrolysis pulse, plus 1.4 s GTP→GDP cues on IF2, EF-Tu and EF-G for GTP events of ≥ 1.0 s.
  - Pure, deterministic and allocation-free (pool on `out._tlPool`). It returns early at t ≤ 0 and t ≥ duration (S2 boundary-defaults test).
- `js/kin/pose.js` — translation parts only (`createTranslationKit`, appended; `initStoryAssembly` / `createPoser` hooks):
  - **Assembly.** When `ribosome-70s`, `trna-init` and `trna-aa` are present, strand 3 adds 120 anticodon nucleotides (role `trna`, indices 675 + 3(k − 1) + q) and 120 codon·anticodon pairs, initially inactive. Without those assets the Assembly keeps the 675-nt transcription topology.
  - **mRNA.** It sits on the ribosome `mrna` track, sliding one register per codon, with 3′ and 5′ free tails (seeded chains). It blends from the chapter-7 pose by `mrna.layout`. A clearance guard pushes mRNA (±30 registers around the track) and anticodons out of L1 beads to ≤ 0.85 Å.
  - **tRNAs.** Rigid tRNA actors are placed with `trnaSiteFrame`. Anticodon nucleotides go through `poseTrnaAnticodon`, and their pairs become active when `pair` > 0. Each tRNA gets an `aa:<slot>` aminoacyl residue group.
  - **Peptide.** The peptide group runs from the peptidyl attach point along the world tunnel path, then on a static sampled free path after release.
  - **Focus and budget.** The focus is the decoding site. Pose median is about 2.6 ms per frame.
- `tests/story-tl.test.mjs` — 11 tests (below).
- `docs/evidence/r3/S4/` — `tl-lab.html` is the evidence harness: the real `StoryScene` with all 12 assets and the chapter-7 clamp lifted (`maxTime` capped to the story duration). Query `?chapter=8..16&frac=` or `?t=`. `capture.mjs` takes the screenshots; there are 18 JPEGs (desktop 1440×900 @2x and iPhone 13, chapters 8–16).
- `docs/handoffs/R3-S4.md` — this record.

## Design gaps

- Two penetrations above 1.0 Å sit outside the item-8 scope (slot tracks, resting tRNAs) and are left as is:
  - anticodons of a tRNA still approaching with EF-Tu reach 1.41 Å (chapter 9, t ≈ 76.17 s);
  - the peptide inside the exit tunnel reaches 1.87 Å.
- The terminator hairpin unfolds as the mRNA blends into the translation layout (the ribosome track needs a linear mRNA).
- GTP cues are skipped for the short (1.1 s) elongation cycles to avoid stacked flashes.
- The 30S approaches from the subunit-interface (+x) side.
- Bond pulses are timed from the bond midpoint, when the residue count changes.
- Asset A1 limitation: the `ribosome-70s` `mrna` track has O3′–P gaps of up to 8.7 Å (register −7), and the free tails show gaps of up to 3.6 Å after clearance pushes.
