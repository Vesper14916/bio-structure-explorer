## Changed files

- `js/scene/crowd.js` — `generateCrowd(scenario, assets, { seed, tier, ribosomePose })`. Seeded random sequential addition (`mulberry32`, default seed 19) in the 1600 × 1000 × 1000 Å box centred on the gene midpoint. Classes: `ribosome-70s` 40, `ef-tu` 96, `rnap-holo` 5 (`round(density × 1.6e-3 µm³)`). Tiers: `high` 100 %, `medium` 50 % (20 / 48 / 3), `low` none. Returns `[{ assetId, transforms: Float32Array (16 floats per instance, column-major), positions, radius }]`, which `viewer.setCrowd` accepts (extra fields are ignored).
- `tests/crowd.test.mjs` — 7 tests: counts and densities, determinism, `medium` is a prefix of `high`, box / capsule / ribosome-sphere exclusion, 0.8 × (r_i + r_j) separation over all 141 crowders, rigid column-major transforms that map the asset centre to the position, random orientations, missing asset and moved ribosome pose.
- `tests/stubs.test.mjs` — `js/scene/crowd.js` marked implemented (DEC-005).
- `docs/SCIENCE.md` — new §5 Crowding.
- `js/scene/story-scene.js` (INT3, Chief-owned) — the scene generates the crowd once per tier and calls `viewer.setCrowd`; the Crowding toggle and the auto quality tier switch it.

## Design gaps

- The brief's box is 1000 Å high and wide, and the capsule radius is 180 Å, so a ribosome crowder (r = 148.7 Å) cannot lie fully inside the box and outside the capsule. The box therefore limits the crowder **centre**; bounding spheres stay outside the capsule and ribosome sphere but may protrude from the box.
- EF-Tu is drawn alone (no tRNA in the ternary complex).
