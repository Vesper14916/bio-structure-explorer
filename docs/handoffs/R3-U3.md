## Not verified

- Real touch gestures (drag of the sheet, swipe), real iOS Safari / WebKit rendering, and the on-screen keyboard behaviour; only Chromium with the iPhone 13 / Pixel 7 profiles was used. The `devices` profiles run in Chromium: `defaultBrowserType` is dropped.
- The full app (`index.html?scene=v2`) was not opened (WebGL, and another agent runs a benchmark). So the claim that the camera honours reduced motion is from reading the source only (see below), not from a running viewer.
- No screen-reader pass (names are computed by the test's own algorithm, not by the browser accessibility tree).
- Colour contrast of the focus ring and of the chapter text was not measured.

## Design gaps

- The scrub range exposes only `aria-valuenow` 0 to 1000; a screen reader announces "260" with no chapter or percentage. An `aria-valuetext` such as "Chapter 6 of 16, 26 percent" is missing. Not changed (`view-story.js` scope here is keyboard and focus).
- The sheet grip has the fixed name "Resize panel" and exposes no state (peek, half, full); `aria-valuetext` or `aria-expanded` would help.
- Chapter rows are 16 separate Tab stops and ArrowUp / ArrowDown do nothing in the list; ArrowLeft / ArrowRight on a focused row steps the chapter (global shortcut), which is acceptable but not a standard list pattern.
- `.range` removes its outline and relies on a thumb ring (`rgba(169,155,255,.35)`, 6 px); visible, but fainter than the 2 px outline everywhere else.
- Reduced-motion static findings (files not owned, read only): `js/render/viewer.js` initialises `this.reducedMotion` from `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` in its constructor (lines 52 to 53) and updates it on the media-query `change` event (line 189 to 190), so it IS initialised from the media query. `moveCamera` forces duration 0 when reduced (line 566); `camera-rails.js` line 488 reads `viewer.reducedMotion` and passes duration 0 (shot transitions are instant cuts); auto-rotate is gated by `!this.reducedMotion` (line 889); cut-away and follow smoothing jump (`k = 1`, lines 380 and 642); particle and molecule motion is multiplied by 0. PLAN 4.7 is therefore met in code. One nuance: with `follow: true` shots the camera still tracks a moving target every frame (rigid follow, no smoothing); that is not an animated transition, but the Chief may want to confirm it is intended under reduced motion.

## Requests

For the Chief (files not owned). Each item names the observed value.

1. **Peek is cut off by the tab bar** (`js/ui/sheet.js` `PEEK_H = 112`, or the peek content). The collapsed sheet shows 112 px but the peek needs 132.8 px (grip 22 + transport 46 + chapter row + 2-line caption), so the second caption line is hidden behind the tab bar and the clamp to 2 lines never shows (see `iphone-13-ch6-collapsed.jpg`: one line visible, cut). Same on Pixel 7 (cut by 20.8 px). Raising `PEEK_H` to about 134 fixes it without touching `--peek-h` (which is 108 px in `css/style.css` and unused; PLAN says it must not change). `tests/ui-phone.browser.mjs` flips this KNOWN-DEFECT to PASS automatically.
2. **Controls are tabbable but invisible while the sheet is collapsed** (`css/style.css`: `.snap-peek .view > :not(.peek) { opacity: 0 }`, `js/ui/sheet.js`). On the phone peek, Tab stops 6 to 32 (26 controls) are focusable but faded out, so a keyboard user focuses invisible controls and the sheet does not expand. Suggest `visibility: hidden` (or `inert`) on those children while `snap-peek`, or expand the sheet on `focusin` inside the panel.
3. **Focus ring clipped** (`css/style.css`): the 2 px outline + 2 px offset is cut by overflow containers. Desktop: 5 stops among the chapter rows (`.chapter-list` has `padding: 0 4px`, no vertical padding; rows at the list's top or bottom edge lose 4 px of ring). Phone: 10 stops (grip, play, restart, segmented options, first chapter row), because `.views` has no top padding on the phone and `.panel-scroll` clips the ring. Suggest `padding: 4px` vertical on `.chapter-list` (with a matching negative margin) and `padding-top: 4px` on `.views` for the phone.
4. **Hit areas below 24 px** (`css/style.css`): `.pep-pill` is 22 px high (min-width 24); `.grip` is 22 px high. Suggest `height: 24px` for the pill and a 24 to 28 px grip (the visible bar can stay 5 px).
5. **Informational, below 44 px**: `.icon-btn-sm` 32x32 ("Previous codon", "Next codon", "Clear selection"), `.chapter-row` 34 px high, `.seg-btn` 40 px high and 41 px wide on iPhone 13 (5-option Camera group).
6. **`dev/ui-story.html`** has no `?fixture=ch11` (falls back to t = 0 for unknown fixtures). The test works around this by clicking chapter row 11.
7. **Viewer**: nothing to change for reduced motion (see Design gaps); `viewer.reducedMotion` is initialised from the media query.

## Open questions

- Should chapter rows become a roving-tabindex list (one Tab stop, Up / Down arrows), like the codon strip now is? It would cut 15 Tab stops; not done because it changes the established row behaviour from U1.
- Should the global ArrowLeft / ArrowRight chapter shortcut also be suspended while a chapter row or the Cutaway tile has focus? Today it fires on any focused button except radios, sliders and tabs.
- Under reduced motion, should a `follow` shot hold the camera still instead of tracking the moving target?
