## Design gaps

- **`8FZH` (`rf1`) 23S-only superposition scope:** Literal `16S+23S` on `8FZH` keeps only `2,069 / 4,272 = 48.4%` (< 50% `DEC-010` gate) due to a +2.68% isotropic cryo-EM magnification offset relative to `7K00`. Fitting on `23S` retains **79.7%** at **1.408 Å** RMSD, anchors `ggq` at `5.961 Å` from `ptc`, and improves `codonReader` to `siteA` (`7.610 Å` vs `7.956 Å`). All alternative scopes are stored in `source.superpositionAlternatives` for ST5 Argon review.
- **`ef-g` Walker A P-loop (`AHIDAGKT`, author 16–23):** *E. coli* EF-G canonically carries Ala at position 1 (`AHIDAGKT` rather than strict `GxxxxGK[ST]`); confirmed 6.576 Å from bound GDP in `7PJV` and 15.313 Å from 23S SRL.
- **`ef-g` `domainIV` residue range:** Author 481–603 (`ASN481..GLY603`, 123 Cα atoms, matching UniProt P0A6M8 structural Domain IV boundaries 482–604).
