## Interface notes for S3 (ST6) and other consumers
- **Frame orientation.** All duplex math runs on coding-oriented base-pair frames `C = partnerFrame(T)`. The template track frames in `rnap-ec` point upstream (measured: template +z · RNAP +z = −0.73…−1.00; `T(r+1) ≈ T(r)·S(−1)`), so the `PLAN.md` §8.3 formulas hold for `C`, not for `T` as written. Template frames are always `partnerFrame(C)`.
- **Frame buffer.** `2·N·16` floats, column-major 4×4 per nucleotide (three.js `Matrix4.elements` order); coding nucleotide `g` at index `g`, its template partner at `N + g`.
- **Machine axis.** Move the RNAP along `downstreamAxis(asset)` (`z(C_{r1})`, 8.13° from the asset +z), not along the asset +z; otherwise downstream DNA cannot stay stationary. `machinePoseForRegister(asset, register)` gives the bound pose at which the unbent duplex coincides with the straight world duplex `W·S(g − 124)`.
- **Bubble.** Whenever `dna.bend > 0`, open the bubble over the hybrid (`dna.bubble` covering at least registers −10…+1). With the bubble closed, the coding strand at registers −9…0 is the partner of the template, which is where the RNA sits; the backbone then breaks by 30–35 Å at the two track kinks. With `[-11, 2]` the coding strand follows the non-template track (max gap 6.6 Å, from the asset).
- **Fifth argument.** `poseDuplex`'s `opts` (`machine`, `trackBlend`) extends the W0 stub signature (see Requests).
- **`rnap-holo`.** It has no tracks yet; `poseDuplex` then uses the `rnap-ec` tracks for both assets. A4 (ST5) adds RPo tracks; the blend is per register where both exist.

## Not verified
- Hardware-GPU rendering; captures use SwiftShader.
- The bubble edge is a hard cut at integer registers, so a base pair crossing the edge while the machine steps changes source frame in one step (up to about 2.5 Å at register −10).
- Allocation was checked with heap growth and by construction (the hot path uses local in-place helpers); no `--expose-gc` measurement for `poseDuplex`.
