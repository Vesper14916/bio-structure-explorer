# Handoff — R3-K3 (tRNA anticodons, aminoacyl ends and codon pairing)

Stage: ST7 · Branch: `r3/K3` (base tag `r3-w2`) · Owner: Claude

## Changed files

- `js/kin/trna.js` — Replaced the W0 stub (PLAN.md §2 D7, §5.1, §5.3 item 6, §7.4):
  - `anticodonOf(codon)` returns the exact 5′→3′ Watson–Crick complement (wobble-free, as §5.1 lists it); `pairedCodonPosition(q) = 2 − q` (nt 34↔3rd, 35↔2nd, 36↔1st codon base); `ANTICODON_NUMBERS = [34, 35, 36]`.
  - `trnaSiteFrame(riboAsset, from, site, blend, out)` interpolates (slerp + lerp) between the `siteAT/siteA/siteP/siteE` anchors of `ribosome-70s`. Hybrid sites `AP`/`PE` use their anchors when the asset has them and otherwise fall back to the midpoint of the two classic sites (the current asset has none). `'out'` resolves to the start site, and the poser adds the departure offset.
  - `poseTrnaAnticodon(trnaSpec, asset, out48)` writes three column-major 4×4 frames for nt 34–36. With `pair = 0` they sit exactly on the asset's `anticodon` track. With `pair = 1` each one is the Watson–Crick partner frame of its codon nucleotide, i.e. the `buildDuplex` complementary-strand construction (the flip-partner frame; the templates give 10.70 Å for A·U and 10.80 Å for G·C). `tightenPair` then pulls C1′–C1′ to ≤ 10.6 Å. Intermediate values slerp continuously. Codon frames are accepted as `{o, R}` objects or as 16-float typed arrays.
  - `poseAminoacylEnd` returns the ester-carbon frame, 1.33 Å (`ESTER_BOND`) along acceptor +z from A76 O3′. `poseAminoacylResidue(spec, asset, aa, group)` fills a one-residue `kind: 'peptide'` group whose carbonyl C sits on the ester carbon. `peptidylAttachPoint` returns that residue's Cα. `pairC1Distances` is the acceptance helper.

- `tests/stubs.test.mjs` — `js/kin/trna.js` marked `implemented: true` (DEC-005 precedent, the same one-line change the Chief made for S3).
