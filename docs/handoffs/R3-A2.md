## Design gaps
- Evidence is stored as JPEG quality 90 at full 2× resolution instead of PNG. The PNG captures were 2.7–3.1 MB each; a 256-colour PNG lost the element colours of the small nucleotides. Chief to confirm the format for later stages.

## Requests
1. **Span range for `trna-aa`.** The plan's 65–80 Å range is exceeded by the 7K00 A-site tRNA (81.7 Å). The value is real: 7K00 Z 76.1, 7ST2 tRNAs 75.3 and 75.5, 5WE4 A/T tRNA 72.6 Å, so the accommodated A-site tRNA in 7K00 is the most extended. Proposal: widen the acceptance to 65–85 Å. The test currently asserts 65–80 Å for init and 65–82 Å for aa, with a comment naming this request.
