# Handoff R3-R3: Camera rails and labels (Stage ST6)

Branch: `r3/r3` (base `431f327`). Spec: `PLAN.md` §§4.6, 4.7, 6.3, 6.4, 8.1, 8.4, 10 (R3-R3); `docs/CONTRACTS.md` §7.5; `docs/DECISIONS.md` `DEC-005`, `DEC-012`.

## Changed files

- `js/scene/camera-rails.js`: Replaced W0 stub with `resolveShot(shotOrId, worldState, assets)`, `resolveCameraShotId(cameraOption, worldState)`, `transitionDuration(fromPos, toPos)`, and `createCameraRails(viewer, opts)`.
  - `resolveShot` resolves all 13 catalog shots (`SHOT_IDS`) across `'world'`, `'rnap'`, `'ribo'`, `'tunnel'` midpoint, and `'coupled'` midpoint (`[rnap('center'), ribo('center')]`) targets; computes unit world view direction `dir`, numeric or `'fit'` camera distance (FOV 32°, 10 % margin for `wide`, 15 % margin for `coupled-wide`/`coupled-follow`, clamped to `[280, 2600] Å`), camera position `pos`, `detailRadius`, `follow`, and cutaway specification (`'activeSite'`, `'rnaExit'`, `'decoding'`, `'ptc'`, `'tunnel-axis'`).
  - `resolveCameraShotId` maps `'overview' -> 'wide'`, `'rnap' -> 'rnap-follow'` (when transcript length > 0) or `'rnap-side'`, `'ribosome' -> 'ribo-side'`, `'auto' -> worldState.shot.id || worldState.chapter.shot || 'wide'`, and `'free' -> null`.
  - `transitionDuration` implements `clamp(0.8 + travel / 600, 1.2, 3.0)` seconds.
  - `createCameraRails` binds `viewer.on('controlstart', ...)` to switch the active option to `'free'` on user orbit/pan/zoom, drives `viewer.moveCamera` (with `duration = 0` when `instant` or `viewer.reducedMotion` is true), updates `viewer.setFollowTarget`, and updates `viewer.setDetailFocus`.
- `js/scene/labels.js`: Replaced W0 stub with `selectLabels(worldState, assets, opts)`, `MAX_VISIBLE_LABELS = 6`, and `LABEL_COLORS`.
  - Evaluates all qualifying scene labels in strict priority order 1 → 9 (`PLAN.md` §4.6):
    1. `RNA polymerase` (`hx-blob`, `#4fb6ffaa`)
    2. `σ⁷⁰` (`hx-blob`, `#b58cffaa`, when visible and opacity > 0.5)
    3. `Ribosome` (overview shots: `wide`, `coupled-wide`, or `cameraOption === 'overview'`) or `30S subunit` and `50S subunit` (all other shots) (`hx-blob`, `#e4dcc4aa` / `#d2b183aa`)
    4. `mRNA 5′` and `mRNA 3′` (`hx-label`, `#2fd9b9aa`, when mRNA length ≥ 12)
    5. `fMet-tRNA` / `<Aa>-tRNA` / `tRNA` (deacylated) (`hx-blob`, `#ffd43baa` / `#ff922baa`)
    6. `EF-Tu`, `EF-G`, `RF1`, `IF1`, `IF2`, `IF3`, `NusG` (`hx-blob`, factor token border colour)
    7. `Peptide N-terminus` (`hx-label`, `#ff7a45aa`, when peptide length ≥ 1)
    8. `DNA 5′`, `DNA 3′` (`hx-label`, `#7c93c3aa`, overview shots only)
    9. `−35`, `−10`, `+1`, `SD`, `AUG`, `UAA`, `Terminator` (`hx-sm`, chapters 1–3, 7 / coupled 10, 8–9 / coupled 7, 15 / coupled 11)
  - Returns `[]` when `opts.enabled === false` and enforces a hard cap of at most 6 labels.
- `js/render/overlay.js`: Added `setLabels(list)` on `OverlayLayer` (enforcing priority sort and hard cap of 6) and 250 ms opacity fade-in (`0 → 1`) and fade-out (`1 → 0`, removing DOM nodes once fade reaches 0) in `updateTags(ctx, t)`. Preserved all existing base labels, distance/text annotations, and Round 1 blob methods intact.
- `tests/camera-rails.test.mjs`: Unit and Playwright browser verification suite testing `transitionDuration`, `resolveCameraShotId`, `resolveShot` across all 13 shots and 5 frame types, `selectLabels` priority ordering and `<= 6` cap across all 16 sequential and 12 coupled chapters, `controlstart` Free interrupt on user canvas orbit, `reducedMotion` instant camera cuts, and 250 ms label fade-in/out in `OverlayLayer`.
- `docs/evidence/r3/R3/`: 2 screenshots at 1440×900 `deviceScaleFactor: 2` JPEG q90 (`shot-rnap-side-labels.jpg`, `shot-wide-overview-labels.jpg`), visually inspected.

## Design gaps

- **Fallback world positions for procedural molecules in `selectLabels`:** Because `WorldState` carries logical state (`rna.length`, `peptide.length`, `dna.register`) while `Poser` (`js/kin/pose.js`) owns the procedural atom buffers, `selectLabels(worldState, assets, opts)` computes deterministic world anchors from machine transforms (`rnaExit`, `activeSite`, `mrnaEntry`, `mrnaExit`, `paths.tunnel`, and Construct v3 base-pair indices) and accepts an optional `opts.positions` map (`{ [labelIdOrText]: [x, y, z] }`) so `StoryScene` (`R3-INT2`) can pass exact posed nucleotide/residue terminal coordinates when available.
