## Design gaps

1. **Pre-existing Step-by-step `attachPt → tunnel[0]` 180° peptide path reversal (`js/kin/pose.js:1280`, R3-S4):** `ribosome-70s.paths.tunnel[0]` (`[69.15, 9.85, -10.3]`) lies `13.47 Å` behind `attachPt` (`[79.5, 2.34, -14.53]`, closest to `tunnel[14]` at `3.52 Å`). Prepending `attachPt` before `tunnel[0]` creates a sharp 180° hairpin that flips RMF residue orientations (`|C–N − 1.33| ≈ 1.12 Å`) in a ~`0.05 s` window around `peptide.release ≈ 0.065`. R3-S5 fixed this for `rna.layout === 'coupled'` while leaving Step-by-step untouched per `DEC-032` minimal-change scope; Chief/V1 may wish to extend the prefix trim to Step-by-step in INT4.
2. **UI segmented control overlap (`css/style.css` / `js/ui/view-story.js`):** Observed in `docs/evidence/r3/S5/` desktop and phone captures: when `Coupled` is selected in the `STORY` segmented control, the `"p"` of `"Step by step"` slightly touches/clips against the active `"Coupled"` button border.
3. **Top-edge label overlap with brand header (`js/scene/labels.js`):** Observed in `phone-ch07.jpg`, `phone-ch10.jpg`, `phone-ch11.jpg`, and `desktop-ch07.jpg`: top-most 3D labels (`30S subunit`, `IF1`, `fMet-tRNA`, `tRNA`) can overlap the top viewport edge or the Helix brand bar (same pre-existing clamping behavior noted in `R3-V2`).
4. **Coupled `mRNA 5′`/`mRNA 3′` label suppression (`js/scene/labels.js:491-493`):** The ST8 `DEC-030` `decodingChapters` suppression checks `worldState.rna?.layout === 'ribosome'` and `chIdx ∈ [10, 14]`, so coupled chapters 9–11 still emit `mRNA 5′`/`mRNA 3′` (priority 4), consuming 2 of the 6 label slots (e.g., crowding out `NusG` in ch 9 when `50S`, `30S`, `RNAP`, `Leu-tRNA`, `mRNA 5′`, `mRNA 3′` fill all 6 slots, as seen in `desktop-ch09.jpg`).
5. **Static crowders vs moving expressome (`js/scene/story-scene.js:241`):** `generateCrowd` uses only `ribosomePose: RIBO_LAYOUT` for the ribosome exclusion sphere; in coupled mode the ribosome travels ~184 Å along DNA with RNAP. No distracting occlusion appeared in ch06–12 captures, though distant crowders sit closer to the moving ribosome in ch08/ch12.
